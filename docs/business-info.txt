Miester's Massage Spa — 1420 North Canyon Road, Provo, Utah.

WHAT WE DO
Swedish massage — relaxation, light to medium pressure. 60 min $85, 90 min $120.
Deep tissue — firm pressure, knots and chronic tension. 60 min $100, 90 min $135.
Prenatal — second and third trimester only, side-lying with bolsters. 60 min $90.
Couples — two therapists, one room, side by side. 60 min $170, 90 min $240.
Hot stone — heated basalt stones, 90 minutes only. $140.
Sports recovery — stretching and trigger point work. 60 min $95, 90 min $130.

ADD-ONS (added to any session)
Aromatherapy $10. CBD balm on problem areas $15. Scalp and neck treatment $15.
Peppermint foot treatment $12.

DEALS
First-time clients get 20 percent off their first session.
Membership is $75 a month and includes one 60-minute massage plus 15 percent off
anything else that month. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice.
Gift cards are available in any amount and never expire.
Packages of five 60-minute sessions are $380, about $76 a session.

HOURS
Monday through Friday, nine in the morning to six in the evening.
Saturday is limited and by request only — we offer ten in the morning to two in
the afternoon when a therapist is available.
Closed Sunday and closed on major holidays.
Last appointment starts one hour before closing for a 60-minute session.

THE TEAM
Marisol — deep tissue and sports recovery, twelve years experience, speaks Spanish.
Dev — Swedish, hot stone, prenatal certified.
Tanya — sports recovery and prenatal, works with runners and post-natal clients.
Clients can request a therapist by name, or ask for whoever is available.

HOW BOOKING WORKS
We take requests by phone and the front desk confirms by text or call, usually
within a few hours during business hours.
We book up to six weeks out.
Saturdays and evenings after four fill up first, often a week ahead.
Same-day is sometimes possible — worth asking, but never promise it.

POLICIES
Please arrive ten minutes early. First visit includes a short intake form.
Late arrivals still end at the scheduled time so the next client is not delayed.
Cancellations need 24 hours notice. Less than 24 hours or a no-show is charged
half the session price.
Tipping is optional and appreciated, cash or card.
We take all major cards, cash, and HSA or FSA cards when a doctor has recommended
massage. We do not bill insurance directly.
Free parking in the lot behind the building.
The studio is on the ground floor and is wheelchair accessible.
Quiet space — we ask that clients keep phones on silent.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO
No medical diagnosis, physical therapy, or emergency care.
Not a medical clinic and we do not write treatment plans or doctor's notes.
Clients with health concerns, pregnancy complications, injuries, or recent surgery
should have a licensed professional review it before booking.
No massage within 72 hours of surgery, or over open wounds, rashes, or active
infections.
First trimester prenatal is declined — we ask clients to wait until 13 weeks.
We are strictly non-sexual. Any inappropriate request ends the call and the client
is refused service permanently.
No one under 16 without a parent present.

COMMON QUESTIONS
"Do I need to undress?" — Clients undress to their comfort level and are draped
with a sheet the entire time. Only the area being worked on is uncovered.
"Will it hurt?" — Deep tissue can be intense but should never be painful. Clients
should tell the therapist to ease up any time.
"How often should I come?" — Most regular clients come every two to four weeks.
"Can I bring my kid?" — There is no childcare and the studio is a quiet space.
"Do you do gift certificates?" — Yes, any amount, they never expire.
